How data is handled

  • Camera runs only during the selected session.
  • Frames are sent for immediate in-memory processing, never written as video files.
  • Counts are aggregate face observations, not unique student counts.
  • When the timer ends, capture stops and the summary remains in MySQL.
  • Facial expressions are uncertain visual estimates, not proof of attention, learning, or mood.
